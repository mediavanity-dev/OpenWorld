OpenEarth is a walking game built from open map and nature data. No account or sign-in is needed.

To try it from a desk (no travel needed):
1. On the start screen, under "explore anywhere in the world (Roam)", tap "Greenwich" (or type any place and tap Go). This puts you in Roam mode there.
2. Tap the gold "Expedition" box (bottom panel), then Accept a quest. Move with the joystick (bottom left); the ring around the character and the radar (top right) point the way. Reaching the goal turns over a card. For a mystery, "I'm here" in the Expedition window finishes it within about 90 m.
3. Tap "Journal" for collections (including the wildlife recorded nearby) and "This spot" for geology, sky and history.
4. Walk mode uses the phone's real location (permission asked when chosen). Camera view (the camera button) works in Walk mode on a phone outdoors; it asks for camera and motion permission.

Location, camera and motion are used only on the device. Only the surrounding area is sent to public data services (OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo, iNaturalist, Wikipedia/Wikidata, USGS) to load map, weather and wildlife data. Nothing is collected by us.

In-app purchases: three optional consumable tips (com.mediavanity.openworld.tip.small, .tip.medium, .tip.large). They unlock nothing. Find them in the bottom panel → "Settings & help" → "Support OpenEarth". No restore is needed for consumables.

Content: map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); wildlife records and photos from iNaturalist (only CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA photos, credited); text from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA, credited) and facts from Wikidata (CC0).

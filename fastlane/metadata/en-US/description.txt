Your street is full of things worth finding. OpenEarth turns a walk into an expedition: riddles about real places, animals people have really seen near you, and races to the high ground before the sun goes down.

Everything is real. The places, the wildlife, the rock under your feet and tonight's sky all come from open data about exactly where you are, drawn as a simple, friendly 3D world.

EXPEDITIONS, DEALT FRESH EVERY DAY
• Mystery: a riddle about a real place nearby. Work out which one and walk there. Stuck? Buy a clue.
• Bounty: a "wanted" animal really recorded around here. Head for its habitat, follow its signs, and walk up to it gently. See a real one? Tap "I saw it!"
• Race: reach the high ground, a viewpoint or the water's edge inside par time, or before the real sunset.
• Water: follow a stream to where it meets a river, or find where it begins.
• Real-world moments: a meteor shower, a full moon, golden hour or rain deal special expeditions and rarer cards.

COLLECT
• Every finish turns over a card. Its rarity comes from real facts: a 300-year-old church, a shy animal, a big climb.
• Wildlife around here: every species recorded near you, as silhouettes until you find them.
• Quick challenges: guess when a building went up, name the bird you spotted, tell whether the rock is older than the dinosaurs.
• A field journal of your places, species, rocks, records and the ground you've walked.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Walk: you are where your phone is, and everything counts.
• Home: wander around your home base from the sofa.
• Roam: visit anywhere on Earth (finds there are echoes until you go for real).
• Camera view: see places and creatures over the real world through your camera.
• Dress your explorer: nine outfits and four hats.

FREE
OpenEarth is free, with no ads and nothing to unlock. Optional tips in Settings help keep it going; they don't change the game.

OPEN DATA
Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Weather from Open-Meteo, wildlife records and photos from iNaturalist (each photo credited), facts from Wikipedia and Wikidata, geology from Macrostrat, earthquakes and US rivers from USGS.

PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your journal stays on your phone; only the area around you is used to fetch map, weather and wildlife data.

Please stay aware of your surroundings while you play.

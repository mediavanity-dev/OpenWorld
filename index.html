    OpenWorld

    The real Earth, abstracted: real places, weather, wildlife and rocks, drawn simply from open data.

    Three ways to play

    Present: you are where your phone really is; walk to move. Finds are claimed.
    Home: roam 600 m around your home base from the sofa. Finds count as home.
    Roam: visit anywhere virtually. Finds are only echoes until you go for real.

    What to do

    Walk within 30 m of a coloured marker to discover a real place (shops and cafés only while they're open). Animals that people have really recorded nearby leave signs as you walk: follow the rustle on the minimap and the 🐾 tracker (warmer or colder) until it shows itself, then reach it or photograph it in camera view. Go gently, or it slips away. Each day three golden finds ✦ are hidden in every area along public paths; the ✦ brightens as you get closer. Where available, real geocaches from Opencaching appear as small box icons: tap one, find the container, then tap “I found it”. The ground beneath you is identified as you move, and each day brings one gentle field assignment.

    Your progress

    📓 Journal: collections, records, surveyed areas and your explored map. 🔍 This spot: geology, sky, climate, history and earthquakes around you. Export your profile to back it up; it lives only on this device.

    Camera view (phones, Present)

    📷 shows labels over the real world through your camera. Hold the phone upright, turn slowly, and drag sideways if labels look off. 📸 saves a photo. The camera image is never recorded or uploaded.

    Controls

    Drag to look (Present: drag to pan) · pinch or scroll to zoom · two-finger twist to rotate · ◎ or C to return to yourself · H hide the interface · tap anything for details.

    Data & privacy

    No account and no tracking. Your position stays on your device; only the surrounding area is used to fetch open data. Your journal and species are never uploaded.
